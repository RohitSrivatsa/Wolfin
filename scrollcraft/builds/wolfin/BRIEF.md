# Wolfinn, website brief

The brand was renamed from Wolfin to **Wolfinn** (new wordmark in `assets/wolfinn-wordmark.png`). The live site is in `/site`.

Interviewed. The answers are the founder's own words, lightly tidied. Status: first build done (`/site`). Founder asked to "keep it simple, not too much text, more visual and interactive", with the gap finder as the centrepiece.

## Direction
- **Chosen:** Waterline, white version (round two, mockup 2). White sky above, the logo's teal water below, the moon, the wolf on its island, and the orca underneath.
- **Grammar:** split stage, laid out horizontally (above the waterline versus below it).
- **Palette:** taken from the logo: ink #0B0D11, moon #F27A2E, tide #0E3A42, deep #032A31, bone #D8D2C6. Light theme.

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** "visually attractive with simplicity and professionalism", "calm and provide a good message".
2. **Journey:** hero, the services, how we work, the founder, and an email ending. Sections still to be worked through "slowly, paragraph by paragraph".
3. **Energy:** calm throughout. Motion is "the water flowing elegantly, along with the whales, fin flapping".
4. **Feeling and peak:** visitors should feel that Wolfin "has the ability to do whatever we want", and come away with confidence in Wolfin. They should remember "the uniqueness that we provide, along with the ability to help integrate technology into their business to make it more efficient, or have some automations that reduce manual work".
5. **Unique thing:** "it should be an experience for them rather than just a website", with "good transitions of pages, along with simplicity and professionalism".
6. **Range:** premium-minimal with editorial type, not maximalist.
7. **World:** distinct scenes (no fly-through), joined by tide transitions.
8. **Assets:** the logo mark, the wordmark and the cut-out layers in `assets/`. No photos yet.

## Business
- **What:** consulting and software solutions; "any sort of help required for any businesses". Starting with local businesses, but the site doesn't need to say so.
- **Services:** still a work in progress. Working list: gap audit, AI assistants and agents, workflow automation, custom software and internal tools, websites and web apps, integrations and data, software consulting, ongoing care and training.
- **Buying:** no prices or quotes on the site. The only action is to email Wolfin; the address is still to come.
- **Belief to install:** "if they have any gaps in their industry, we can help sort it out", and if they feel technology could help their business, Wolfin can make it happen.
- **Proof:** none yet. No invented numbers, logos or testimonials.
- **Brand:** the founder is the brand, a solo practice. No location shown.
- **Hosting:** not decided yet.

## Feeling curve (draft)
| Stage | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Calm, curiosity | White sky, still moon, water drifting, the orca's tail beating |
| Gap finder | Recognition | Their own manual task sinks below the waterline and comes back as a system |
| Services | Assurance | Each service shown above (what they notice) and below (what runs) |
| Founder | Trust | One person, plain words, nothing inflated |
| Close | Resolve | The tide rises, the email address sits underwater, ready to copy |

**Peak:** the gap finder. "You pick the boring job you do every week, it sinks under the water and comes back up as something that does itself."

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where you drop your most annoying task into the water and watch it come back automated.
