Which I'd pick, and a hybrid

B, Waterline, is the one only Wolfin could have. It comes straight out of your mark, it explains what an AI agency does in a single picture, and it stays short. It is also the easiest to keep premium, because the motion is a slow tide rather than a flythrough.

The strongest hybrid: B's structure with A's voice. Waterline layout, but set with an editorial serif for the big statements and written like Field Notes. C's running exhibits could live inside B as the "below the surface" layer for each service.
