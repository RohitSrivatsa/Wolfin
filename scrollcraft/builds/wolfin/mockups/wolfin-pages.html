More ideas to choose from

The moon keeps real time

The moon's height follows the visitor's local time of day: low in the morning, high at midday. Nobody is told; returning visitors just notice it's different.

Ripples under the cursor

Moving the mouse across the water leaves faint rings that fade. On a phone, a tap does it. Tiny, calm, very "experience".

The orca follows the scroll

The orca swims a little ahead of where you're reading, then rests under the section you stop on. A quiet guide in place of a progress bar.

The wolf howls once

When someone copies your email, the wolf lifts its head into the howl, a single moment of celebration. No sound.

Type your own gap

On the real gap finder, visitors can type any task. It sinks, and the page sketches a plausible system for it. Later, the description could go straight into the email.

A "gap list" to take away

Visitors tick the chores that sound familiar and get a short summary to paste into their email. Their first message to you is already specific.
