Waterline, round two

The same idea worked several ways, now with the wolf on its island and the orca, cut out of your logo as separate layers. Every mockup on this page is live: the water moves and the orca's tail beats.

Does a white background work?

Yes, and it may be the more "trusted" of the two. White reads as calm and professional, closer to a consultancy than a tech startup, and it makes the moon, wolf and teal water feel like a printed emblem. Night feels more dramatic and more "AI". One rule for white: orange stays on shapes (moon, links, the fin). It's too light for body text on white.

Can the orca move?

Yes, and it already does here. I split the orca into body and tail so the tail can beat on a hinge, and the whole animal drifts slowly. For the real site I'd redraw the orca and wolf as clean vectors. That opens up more: the tail flex bending smoothly, the orca surfacing on scroll, the wolf's head lifting into the howl. All slow and occasional, never a loop that pulls focus.
