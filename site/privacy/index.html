Privacy

Plain English, short. Last updated November 2026.

What this site collects

Visit statistics. We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, approximate location (city or country), device and browser, and how you found us. It uses cookies. We don't use it to identify you, and we don't sell or share this data.

Messages you send. When you use the Contact page, your name, email, business name and message are sent to admin@wolfinn.org through Google Workspace so we can reply. They're only used to answer you.

Your settings. If you switch to dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser. It never leaves your device.

Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser extension that blocks analytics; the site still works. To ask what we hold about you, or to have your messages deleted, email admin@wolfinn.org.
